Already have a walking tour? Make it playable.

Many towns and museums already publish a printed or online walking tour. We can turn your existing tour into a game: each scene is tied to one of your numbered stops, with its address, a QR sign at the building, a “walk to the next stop” map, and an “Open in Google Maps” link for every stop. Your tour's facts stay the source, cited on every screen, and visitors finish by walking the real route. The Woodstock demo works this way, using stops from the Historic Downtown Woodstock Walking Tour.

What you get

  • A branching story (about 5 scenes, 3–5 choices, 2–3 endings) adapted from your archive and approved by your curators
  • Your branding, images and credits, plus a source list on every fact
  • Mobile-first, accessible design (screen-reader friendly, large tap targets, works on spotty Wi-Fi)
  • Scenes linked to your walking-tour stops, with a stop map and Google Maps links
  • QR code signage files and hosting setup on your website or a subdomain
  • Two feedback rounds and testing on real phones
  • A teacher-friendly version for school groups
